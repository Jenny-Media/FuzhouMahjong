# Table artwork

These assets were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. The tabletop and tile backs are used in the live app. The hand sprites document earlier animation designs.

## Table

[Asset](App/Assets.xcassets/WalnutTable.imageset/art.jpg)

Use case: photorealistic-natural. iPhone mahjong game tabletop plate, straight overhead orthographic shot, landscape 4:3. A real traditional mahjong table with deep tea-green woven wool felt and a narrow dark walnut raised rim with gently rounded inside corners at the extreme four edges; the felt covers 93% of the image. Full bleed, centered symmetrical edge-to-edge crop, no area beyond the table. Discreet seam and wear, fine real felt grain at the center, soft even daylight, subtle shadow where the felt meets the rail. Dark jade felt so light gameplay marks read well. Empty surface for a native game UI. No tiles, people, lettering, marks, pockets, graphic patterns, or logos.

## Shuffle hand

[Asset](App/Assets.xcassets/ShuffleHand.imageset/art.png)

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Single isolated adult LEFT hand and short dark charcoal cuff, an overhead sprite for sweeping mahjong tiles. The wrist enters horizontally FROM THE LEFT, fingers pointing right, palm face down viewed strictly from directly overhead, five naturally relaxed fingers spread slightly with gaps. NO tiles and nothing in the hand. Realistic hand anatomy and texture, diffuse soft daylight, sharp silhouette. Fully contained horizontal cutout on genuine transparent alpha, absolutely no floor, white backing, white haze, shadow, additional object, or typography. This isolated hand will be mirrored and animated against a separately supplied green tabletop.

## Draw hand

[Asset](App/Assets.xcassets/DrawHand.imageset/art.png)

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Single isolated cutout for a Chinese mahjong game pickup animation, strict overhead orthographic close shot. One anatomically correct adult hand, natural proportions and a dark neutral sleeve, wrist entering from the bottom, thumb and bent forefinger gently gripping the short edges of a single authentic rectangular mahjong tile at its tip in the upper third of the image. The tile has a dark jade green unmarked back facing the camera, with a subtle ivory porcelain bevel. All hand and tile fully inside frame, narrow portrait composition, transparent background. Gentle diffuse daylight, realistic skin and ceramic. No table, surface, environment, additional hands, loose tiles, typography or graphics.

## Tile back

[Asset](App/Assets.xcassets/JadeTileBack.imageset/art.png)

Use case: product-mockup. Single photographic game sprite, one authentic Chinese mahjong tile face down, directly overhead orthographic, vertical 5:7 shape. Dark desaturated jade green solid bakelite rectangular back with small rounded corners; a fine warm ivory porcelain bevel along all edges, slight real thickness along the lower edge, crisp but soft daylight. Object fills the frame with a tiny uniform transparent margin. Genuinely transparent background with no cast shadow, letters, symbols, floor, or other objects. This is a realistic tile back for a premium tea-green tabletop game.
